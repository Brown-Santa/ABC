FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Do you guarantee EB-1A approval?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee a USCIS decision. We focus on the quality and clarity of the work we agree to deliver.

Are you a law firm?

No. We provide profile development and documentation support. Legal advice and filing are handled by your licensed immigration attorney.

Why start with the FOE Trial Pack?

Your Field of Expertise shapes everything else. Getting it right first means every later step is focused, and you see how we work before committing further.

Can I pick only one or two criteria?

Yes. That’s the whole idea. You choose the criteria you want help with, and each one is scoped and priced separately.

How long does each criterion take?

Usually 1 to 3 months, depending on the criterion and how quickly third parties like publishers respond.

What’s included in the evaluation report?

A score for each of the 10 criteria, what evidence you have, what’s missing and clear recommendations. See a sample report.
